# Journey duck icons: departure and arrival

Mode: built-in imagegen, transparent_background: true. Original generated PNG alpha preserved; no raster editing or resizing.

Each role has a separately drawn departure and destination illustration. Departure uses a large forward arrow from a starting circle; destination uses a checkered finish flag and stopped/celebrating pose.

White card borders remain unchanged.

## Assets and exact prompts

Reference: journey-location-driver-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. NEW mobile UI icon in the exact cute polished 3D white duck mascot identity and green/yellow materials of the supplied reference. This is a new icon, not a screenshot. Truly transparent square background, no words, no letters, no logos, no buildings or scenery, complete uncropped whole characters. Centered bold silhouette filling 90% of frame, optimized for viewing at only 66px. Keep white round body, orange beak/feet, rosy cheeks and expressive eyes. A tiny mint oval ground is allowed. One emerald green helmet/jacket DRIVER duck with a compact mint scooter; keep its green role unmistakable. This is the ORIGIN / DEPARTURE icon. The character(s) are setting off, clearly moving or taking an energetic step to the RIGHT, ready to travel. Add ONE LARGE thick 3D forward arrow emerging from a small hollow starting circle in the foreground at foot level. This arrow is an unambiguous departure cue, occupies about one third of the icon, and remains readable at 66px. Use mint/emerald for the driver arrow, sunny yellow for passenger, emerald with yellow accent for shared. Do NOT include a flag, checkmark, checkered pattern, or map pin.
