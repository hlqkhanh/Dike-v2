# Journey location duck icons

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, with transparent_background enabled.

Character style references: green ride duck.png and yellow waiting duck.png from apps/web/public/images.

The original generated PNG alpha is preserved. No image resizing or raster edits were applied.

## Role mapping

- Driver: green helmet and jacket, mint scooter.
- Passenger: yellow cap and backpack.
- Shared stop: both mascots together at one pin.
- The same role icon is used for origin and destination; the field caption and route order distinguish them.
- Role comes from the matched people, including reversed owner/requester roles on NEED_RIDE posts.
